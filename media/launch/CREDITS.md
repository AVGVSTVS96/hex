# Credits

## Music

`music.m4a` is a Suno v6 instrumental, slowed from 125 to 120 BPM and cut on its beats to the scenes. Its prompt:

> Instrumental only. 120 BPM, C major, 4/4. Crisp, elegant, high-energy French house and nu-disco for a sleek software launch film. Punchy tight kick, snappy clap on 2 and 4, shuffling open hats, rubbery funky bassline, filtered disco chord stabs, bright plucked synth hook, glassy bell accents. Clean, polished, modern mix with lots of air. Opens with only filtered chord stabs and a soft pulse for 4 bars, a short riser, then the full groove drops in. Changes one layer every 8 bars. A stripped breakdown of just chords near the end, a final lift with everything, and it ends on one clean chord stab that rings out.
